:::caution[Today]
There is no sign-in and no access level yet: anyone who has the address of a Window can open it, and addresses can be guessed. Publish nothing that you would not share by link.
:::

The access of a Window says who can open it. Access levels come with accounts, after the first slice. This page describes them as planned.

## Four levels

Each Window will open to one of:

| Access | Who opens the Window |
| --- | --- |
| Room only | The members of its Room, signed in. The default. |
| Internal | Everyone signed in to its Cahut. |
| Key | Anyone who types its Key. |
| Public | Anyone who has its address. |

An access can end on a date, which can be pushed back. A Window that its reader may not open answers like a Window that does not exist, `404 Not Found`, with a link to sign in.

## Keys

A Key is a password that opens one Window. The reader types it on a page of Cahut, in the house style of the Cahut, and Cahut keeps only a hash of it.

## Who changes the access

Narrowing the access of a Window can be undone, so an AI will do it on its own. Making a Window public is one of the actions that wait for a person: the AI will knock first, and a person will approve or decline. See [Knock first and the Logbook](/concepts/knock-first-and-logbook/).

## The sandbox stays

Whatever its access, every Window runs in a sandbox, apart from the other Windows and from the reader's session. A protected Window will open through a page of Cahut that checks its reader, then shows the Window in a sandboxed frame. See [The sandbox](/concepts/rooms-and-windows/#the-sandbox).
