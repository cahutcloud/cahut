Cahut speaks the language of the house, in plain English. These words mean the same thing in these pages, on the website, and in the tools that an AI sees.

<div class="cahut-term">
<CahutPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Cahut

An instance on your server, at its own domain, such as `cahut.example.com`. One server can hold several Cahuts, and each has its own people, Windows, tokens and Logbook. Cahut, with a capital, is also the name of the product. See [Cahuts and servers](/concepts/cahuts/).

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<RoomPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Room

A personal or team space, with its members, at `<domain>/<room>`. Each person has a personal Room, named after their handle, where their AI publishes by default. A team Room gathers its owners and members. See [Rooms and Windows](/concepts/rooms-and-windows/).

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<WindowPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Window

A living page: a document, deck, dashboard or app, at `<domain>/<room>/<window>`. Today a Window is a static page or site, made of HTML and its files, that an AI publishes over MCP. Apps come later. See [Publish a Window](/connect/publish/).

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<KeyPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Key

The password that opens a protected Window. Keys come with the access levels of Windows. See [Who can open a Window](/concepts/access/).

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<HouseStylePictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## House style

The organization's brand, applied by every AI: its colors, fonts, logo and templates. Each Cahut will have its own, which Cahut gives to the AIs that publish there. It comes later.

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<KnockFirstPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Knock first

The rule that the AI asks before any irreversible action, and that a person approves it. See [Knock first and the Logbook](/concepts/knock-first-and-logbook/).

</div>
</div>

<div class="cahut-term">
<LogbookPictogram class="cahut-pictogram" aria-hidden="true" />
<div>

## Logbook

The record of every action: which AI or person acted, on whose behalf, what they did and with what result. Each Cahut has one, and so does the server. See [Knock first and the Logbook](/concepts/knock-first-and-logbook/#the-logbook).

</div>
</div>

## Writing these words

Cahut, Room, Window and Key take a capital in running text: "3 Rooms in your Cahut", "This Key no longer opens Q3 review." The word for an instance is a Cahut, with a capital, like the product.

A Window opens to its Room only, to everyone signed in to its Cahut, to anyone with its Key, or to the public: that is the access of a Window.
