Cahut is the home for everything your AIs make: documents, slide decks, dashboards and small sites, published to your own server and shared by link.

An AI publishes a page to your Cahut over MCP and gets its address back. You share the link instead of a file. Every call the AI makes goes to the Logbook.

:::note[Early development]
Cahut is in early development. These pages describe what works today, and say so when something is only planned. [Where Cahut stands](/start/status/) lists both.
:::
