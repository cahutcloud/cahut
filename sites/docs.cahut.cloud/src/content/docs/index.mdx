Cahut is a home to publish and share what your AIs make: documents, slide decks, dashboards and small sites, as pages on your own server, shared by link.

An AI publishes a page to your Cahut over MCP and gets its address back. You share the link instead of a file, and your team works on it in Rooms, with its AIs. Every call the AI makes goes to the Logbook. [What Cahut can and cannot do](/start/can-and-cannot/) says what fits in a Window, before you try.

:::note[Early development]
Cahut is in early development. These pages describe what works today, and say so when something is only planned. [Where Cahut stands](/start/status/) lists both.
:::

:::tip[No server?]
Try Cahut on [My Cahut](/start/my-cahut/), at `my.cahut.cloud`: sign up, then connect Claude from its directory of connectors, with no address to copy.
:::
