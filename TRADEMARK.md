# Using the name and the logo

The code of Cahut is free software under the AGPL-3.0. The name "Cahut" and the Cahut logo are not: they identify this project, and the license grants no right to them. The project intends to register the name as a trademark; this policy applies either way. It follows what Mastodon and Nextcloud do, and it protects one thing: that "Cahut" means the software maintained here, under its promises.

## Without asking

You may:

- say that something **runs on Cahut**, **works with Cahut** or **publishes to Cahut**;
- name Cahut to describe it, compare it, review it, teach it or write about it;
- distribute **unmodified** copies of Cahut under its name, as release archives, packages or container images, as long as you point to this repository and keep the license with them;
- self-host Cahut for yourself or for your organization, and say that you do;
- use the logo as it is, unchanged, to point to Cahut, as the [brand guidelines](docs/charte-graphique/README.md) describe it.

## Not without a written agreement

You may not:

- use "Cahut" or the logo in the name of your product, service, company, domain or social account, or in any way that suggests the project endorses you or runs your service;
- keep the name or the logo on a **modified** version. A fork takes another name and another logo; it may say that it derives from Cahut;
- alter the logo: recolor it, redraw it, add to it or combine it with another mark;
- sell hosting, support or training under the Cahut name.

For an agreement, or for any doubt, write to `contact@cahut.cloud`. Hosting partners who install Cahut for their customers are welcome: that is exactly what the agreement is for.
