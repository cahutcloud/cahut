# Getting help

Three places, in this order:

1. **The documentation**, at [docs.cahut.cloud](https://docs.cahut.cloud): installing, connecting an AI, the concepts, administration, and the reference of every operation and command.
2. **The Discussions**, in *Q&A*: ask there. Other users answer, and the maintainer passes by once a week.
3. **An issue**, for a bug you can reproduce, with the bug report form.

There is no support by e-mail or by private message, and no guaranteed response time. Cahut is maintained by one person, in about one slot a week. The only private addresses are `security@cahut.cloud` for vulnerabilities ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)), `conduct@cahut.cloud` for the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), and `contact@cahut.cloud` for the name and partnerships ([TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)). None of them does support.

## Asking well

Give:

- the version, from `cahut version`;
- the system: AlmaLinux or Ubuntu, and its version;
- how Cahut was installed: `install.sh`, a release archive, or `make dev` on your machine;
- the AI client you connect with, if it plays a part;
- what you expected, and what happened;
- the lines of the Logbook that go with it, from `cahut logbook`, without any token or password.

## What to expect

Every new issue gets an answer within a week: confirmed, needs details, duplicate, out of scope, or accepted and placed in a milestone. That is not a promise of a fix. An issue waiting for details for thirty days is closed, and reopens with a comment. A confirmed bug is never closed for inactivity.

Paid support for organizations may come after version 1.0. It will never come at the expense of the public answers.
